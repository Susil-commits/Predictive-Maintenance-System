# Standard Operating Procedure: Overstrain Failure & Hydraulic Overpressure (OSF)

## 1. Overview & Physical Mechanism
Overstrain Failure (OSF) manifests when dynamic structural or hydraulic loading exceeds the yield limit of mechanical components or hydraulic circuit design tolerances. It typically occurs when elevated hydraulic/pneumatic pressure coincides with high mechanical resistance or tool feed engagement, triggering catastrophic structural fatigue, gasket rupture, or micro-cracking in load-bearing brackets.

## 2. Telemetry Symptoms & Risk Signatures
- **Primary Indicator**: Operating pressure exceeding rated nominal range (P > 30.0 bar; transient surge P > 40.0 bar).
- **Secondary Correlation**: Elevated overstrain index (interaction of pressure and vibration amplitude).
- **Physical Clues**: Hydraulic line pulsation, acoustic hammering sounds, cylinder seal seepage, and frame stress marks.

## 3. Diagnostic & Inspection Protocol
1. **Pressure Transducer & Gauge Audit**: Connect a calibrated master digital pressure gauge to the main hydraulic manifold; record pressure peak profiles across full stroke.
2. **Relief Valve Functionality**: Measure pilot stage response time and crack pressure on primary and secondary proportional relief valves.
3. **Accumulator Pre-Charge Verification**: Check nitrogen pre-charge pressure in hydraulic accumulators using standard Schrader charging manifold.
4. **Structural NDT Inspection**: Inspect mechanical load frame, carriage guide blocks, and bracket welds using dye-penetrant or ultrasonic testing for stress micro-fractures.

## 4. Probable Root Causes
- Proportional relief valve sticking or contaminated with particulate varnish.
- Nitrogen bladder leakage in hydraulic accumulator, destroying dampening capacity during valve closure.
- Unscheduled mechanical binding in slide guides or kinematic linkages.
- Excessive workpiece cut depth or uncalibrated feed rates causing mechanical stall resistance.

## 5. Prescriptive Remediation & Corrective Actions
- **Immediate (Safety)**: Lower operating line pressure and clamp cylinder load by 15–25% to stay below PR fatigue threshold.
- **Valve Overhaul**: Disassemble, clean, and lap the main pressure relief valve spool; replace worn pilot seat seals.
- **Accumulator Re-charge**: Re-charge hydraulic nitrogen bladders to 80% of nominal minimum system operating pressure.
- **Structural Reinforcement**: Re-torque frame foundation anchor bolts and align linear guide rails within 0.02 mm parallelism.
