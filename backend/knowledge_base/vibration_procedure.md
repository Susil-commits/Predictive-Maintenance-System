# Standard Operating Procedure: Vibration Fatigue & Bearing Wear (VIB)

## 1. Overview & Physical Mechanism
Vibration Fatigue occurs when rotational dynamic imbalance, shaft misalignment, or bearing raceway defect frequencies excite natural structural resonances of the machine assembly. Persistent elevated vibration induces cyclic micro-strain, accelerating subsurface fatigue spalling in rolling element bearings, loosening foundation bolts, and degrading shaft seal integrity until mechanical failure occurs.

## 2. Telemetry Symptoms & Risk Signatures
- **Primary Indicator**: Vibration amplitude exceeding ISO 10816 Zone C/D limits (vibration > 0.45 mm/s RMS; critical hazard > 0.70 mm/s).
- **Secondary Correlation**: Elevated high-frequency spectral energy, vibration wear index, and spectral centroid shift.
- **Physical Clues**: Audible grinding/rumbling noise, fretting corrosion on bearing journals, foundation bolt looseness, and excessive housing heat.

## 3. Diagnostic & Inspection Protocol
1. **FFT Spectral & Demodulation Analysis**: Collect triaxial accelerometer readings (radial horizontal, radial vertical, and axial). Check for fundamental 1X shaft imbalance peak, 2X misalignment harmonic, or high-frequency bearing fault defect frequencies (BPFO, BPFI, BSF, FTF).
2. **Bearing Lubrication & Grease Condition**: Sample grease from bearing housing drain; inspect for bronze/steel flake spalling and black carbonized oil degradation.
3. **Soft-Foot & Foundation Rigidity**: Measure machine foot lift with magnetic dial indicator while loosening anchor bolts individually (foot movement > 0.05 mm indicates soft-foot).
4. **Shaft & Rotor Imbalance Check**: Perform single-plane or two-plane field dynamic balancing check using strobe tachometer and phase reference.

## 4. Probable Root Causes
- Subsurface fatigue spalling or brinelling on rolling element bearing inner/outer raceways.
- Angular or parallel shaft misalignment between motor and driven shaft.
- Rotor mass unbalance caused by uneven material buildup or missing balance weights.
- Inadequate or contaminated bearing lubrication resulting in boundary metal-to-metal contact.

## 5. Prescriptive Remediation & Corrective Actions
- **Immediate (Safety)**: Reduce operational rotational speed by 20% to step down from resonant frequency band and halt further bearing degradation.
- **Lubrication Maintenance**: Purge old grease and replenish with high-performance synthetic polyurea bearing grease (NLGI Grade 2) per manufacturer fill quantity.
- **Bearing Replacement**: If BPFO/BPFI defect harmonics exceed 2.5 mm/s, replace bearing assembly (SKF/FAG explorer grade) using induction bearing heater.
- **Laser Alignment & Balancing**: Realign driving and driven shafts to within 0.03 mm tolerance; dynamically balance rotor to ISO 1940 Grade G2.5 standard.
